# AgriConnect AI --- Project Overview

## 1. Project Title

**AgriConnect AI: An Intelligent Direct Farmer-to-Buyer Marketplace with
AI-Powered Crop Intelligence and Smart Selling Decision Support**

## 2. Project Summary

AgriConnect AI is a software-only, Java full-stack web application that
connects farmers directly with buyers. Farmers can publish available
agricultural products, manage listings, and receive AI-assisted
information to help them decide what to sell and when. Buyers can
discover products by category and location, view product details, and
place orders. An administrator can oversee users, listings, and platform
activity.

The project combines a digital marketplace with crop and market
intelligence. AI-based functions should be introduced in stages,
beginning with a clearly labelled prototype or baseline model and
improving when reliable data becomes available.

## 3. Problem Statement

Farmers may have difficulty finding buyers, comparing market prices,
understanding demand, and deciding when or where to sell. Buyers may
need to search across multiple sources to find products, quantities, and
locations. A centralized digital platform can reduce these information
gaps and make the buying and selling process easier to manage.

## 4. Proposed Solution

Build a responsive website that provides: - Farmer and buyer
registration and login. - Role-based dashboards. - Agricultural product
listings and search. - Category and location filters. - Order placement
and status tracking. - Market-price information. - AI-assisted crop
insights, price estimates, and selling recommendations. - An
administrator area for managing platform records.

## 5. Project Objectives

1.  Connect farmers and buyers through one online marketplace.
2.  Make agricultural listings easier to publish, discover, and compare.
3.  Support informed selling decisions with understandable market
    information.
4.  Provide a simple and accessible interface for users with different
    levels of digital experience.
5.  Protect account information and validate submitted data.
6.  Build the system in modules so it can be tested and extended.

## 6. Intended Users

### Farmers

Create an account, publish products, manage quantity and price, review
orders, and view crop/market insights.

### Buyers

Browse products, filter by crop category and location, view listing
details, place orders, and track order status.

### Administrator

Review users and listings, manage categories and platform data, and
monitor orders or reported issues.

## 7. Scope

### Included in the initial project

-   Responsive web interface.
-   Registration, login, and role-based access.
-   Farmer product management.
-   Buyer marketplace and ordering.
-   Basic admin management.
-   Market-price information module.
-   AI feature prototypes with clear assumptions and limitations.
-   Testing and deployment preparation.

### Out of scope unless added later

-   Hardware, IoT sensors, or physical devices.
-   Real payment processing and bank integrations.
-   Guaranteed live market prices without a trusted data source.
-   Automated agricultural diagnosis presented as professional advice.
-   Logistics, transport booking, or government-service integrations.

## 8. Expected Outcome

A working web application in which a farmer can register, add a product,
and manage orders; a buyer can discover and order a product; and users
can access market and AI-assisted information from their dashboards.

## 9. Success Criteria

-   A new user can register and log in with valid credentials.
-   Users can access only the functions permitted for their role.
-   Farmers can create, edit, view, and remove their own listings.
-   Buyers can search/filter listings and place an order.
-   Order status changes are saved and displayed correctly.
-   Forms show helpful validation and error messages.
-   Core pages work on desktop and mobile-sized screens.
-   AI outputs show their source, assumptions, or prototype status and
    are not presented as guaranteed predictions.
